Belltide — games for Android

This Privacy Policy explains how Belltide ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you play any game we publish under the Belltide developer name on Google Play (each, the "Game"), and when you visit mega-rabbit.com (the "Site").

By downloading, installing, or playing a Game, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

About the name. Belltide is the Google Play developer name used by Mega Rabbit, an independent game studio in the Republic of Korea. Our games and this Policy are hosted on the studio's domain, mega-rabbit.com. Belltide and Mega Rabbit are the same operator.

The short version

  • What we collect: your device and app details, how you play, and an advertising ID. We never see your name, home address, or payment card details.
  • No account: there is nothing to sign up for. The Game gives itself an anonymous ID, and your progress is saved on your device — uninstalling the Game deletes it for good.
  • Ads: our games show ads through AppLovin MAX, which runs an auction among several ad networks — Google AdMob among them. In Europe and the UK we ask your permission first.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles the money. We only get a receipt so we can hand you the item.
  • Deleting everything: email us using Settings → Send Feedback in the Game — see section 9.
  • What we don't do: we don't sell your personal data, and our games aren't built for children under 14.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • No account, no sign-in. Our games do not ask you to register, and there is no account to log in to. When a Game first runs, Firebase Authentication issues it an anonymous ID — a random string tied to that installation. It is not connected to your Google account and tells us nothing about who you are. We use it only to link our own records to that installation, and it is what the Game shows as your player ID.
  • Game and save data. Level progress, in-game currency and items, story and chapter progress, settings, and similar gameplay state are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot read them. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, this progress is permanently lost — there is no cloud backup to restore from.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information. Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, language, country/region, time zone, app version, and mobile network type.
  • Identifiers. The anonymous Firebase Authentication ID described above, a Firebase installation ID, and the Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Usage data. Sessions, levels started and completed, features used, in-game events, and approximate play time.
  • Error events. When the Game hits an error, Google Analytics for Firebase records an error event alongside the app version and device information above. We do not use a crash-reporting SDK and do not collect stack traces, memory dumps, or performance traces.
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city or country level) location may be derived. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

2.3 Purchase information

Purchases are processed by Google Play, not by us. We receive a purchase or transaction token, an order ID, the product ID, and the purchase status, so we can deliver your item and handle support requests.

We never receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or billing address. Those are handled entirely by Google. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Advertising

Our games display advertising through AppLovin MAX, an ad mediation platform. MAX runs an auction among several advertising networks for each ad slot and shows the winning ad. Google AdMob is one of those networks; others taking part include Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Pangle, DT Exchange, and Liftoff.

The set of networks integrated through MAX changes from time to time, so we do not treat the list above as fixed. The current, authoritative list of our authorized advertising partners is published at mega-rabbit.com/app-ads.txt.

To serve ads, AppLovin and the participating networks may access your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and information about how you interact with ads. Each of these companies decides for itself how it uses what it receives, and is responsible for that data under its own privacy policy.

4.1 If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

Before any personalized advertising or non-essential data processing takes place, we show a consent message using AppLovin's consent management platform (CMP), delivered through the AppLovin MAX SDK. You choose there whether to allow personalized ads, and your choice is passed on to the participating ad networks.

The consent message appears when you first run the Game, and the Game does not currently include a screen for reopening it. To change your answer afterwards, use the device-level controls in section 4.3, or email us at hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com — we will act on a withdrawal of consent.

4.2 If you are in California or another US state with privacy legislation

Sharing your advertising identifier with ad partners for personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under those laws. The Game does not currently include its own opt-out screen, so you can opt out by enabling the device-level setting described below, or by emailing us at hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com.

4.3 Controlling advertising on your device

  • Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads from the Game — you will simply see less relevant ones.

9. Data deletion

There is no account to close, and your progress never leaves your device, so deletion has two separate parts:

  • Your game progress. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, removes it permanently. We hold no copy and cannot restore it for you.
  • The records we hold. Email hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com and ask us to delete the anonymous ID for your installation and everything linked to it. The simplest way is Settings → Send Feedback in the Game: it opens an email with your player ID already attached, and we need that ID to find your records.

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted: the anonymous identifier for your installation, the analytics and usage records linked to it, and your support correspondence.

What we may keep: transaction records that we are legally required to retain for tax and commerce purposes (5 years), and aggregated or anonymized statistics that can no longer be linked to you.

What we cannot delete: your Google account and your Google Play purchase history belong to Google, not to us. Data already received by the advertising partners in section 6 is held by those companies under their own policies — their policies explain how to make a request to them.

Deleting your data does not refund unused in-app purchases, and progress cannot be restored afterwards. If you play more than one of our games, each installation has its own anonymous ID — tell us which ones, or say "all games".

11. Children

Our games are intended for players aged 14 and over and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14 (or under the minimum age required for consent in your country, where that age is higher).

If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child below the applicable age without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com.

12. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls limiting who on our side can reach player data, and reliance on Google Cloud infrastructure for the limited records we hold. Your game progress is not part of this — it stays on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
