This Privacy Policy explains how Belltide ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you play any game we publish under the Belltide developer name on Google Play (each, the "Game"), and when you visit mega-rabbit.com (the "Site").

By downloading, installing, or playing a Game, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

About the name. Belltide is the Google Play developer name used by Mega Rabbit, an independent game studio in the Republic of Korea. Our games and this Policy are hosted on the studio's domain, mega-rabbit.com. Belltide and Mega Rabbit are the same operator.

The short version

  • What we collect: your device and app details, how you play, crash reports, and an advertising ID. We never see your name, home address, or payment card details.
  • Ads: our games show ads through Google AdMob. In Europe and the UK we ask your permission first, and you can change that answer any time in the Game's settings.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles the money. We only get a receipt so we can hand you the item.
  • Deleting everything: you can wipe your account and data from inside the Game, or by emailing us — see section 9.
  • What we don't do: we don't sell your personal data, and our games aren't built for children under 14.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Platform account sign-in. If you sign in to save your progress, we receive a player identifier from Google Play Games and, depending on your settings, your display name and avatar. We never receive your password, and we do not create accounts of our own.
  • Game and save data. Level progress, in-game currency and items, story and chapter progress, achievements, settings, and similar gameplay state.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information. Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, language, country/region, time zone, app version, and mobile network type.
  • Identifiers. An app-generated pseudonymous player ID, a Firebase installation ID, and the Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Usage data. Sessions, levels started and completed, features used, in-game events, and approximate play time.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. Stack traces, error logs, device state at the time of a crash, and performance metrics.
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city or country level) location may be derived. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

2.3 Purchase information

Purchases are processed by Google Play, not by us. We receive a purchase or transaction token, an order ID, the product ID, and the purchase status, so we can deliver your item and handle support requests.

We never receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or billing address. Those are handled entirely by Google. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Advertising

Our games display advertising through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and information about how you interact with ads.

4.1 If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

Before any personalized advertising or non-essential data processing takes place, we show a consent message using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose whether to allow personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings → Privacy options screen.

4.2 If you are in California or another US state with privacy legislation

Sharing your advertising identifier with ad partners for personalized advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under those laws. You can opt out at any time via Settings → Privacy options in the Game, or by enabling the device-level setting described below.

4.3 Controlling advertising on your device

  • Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads from the Game — you will simply see less relevant ones.

9. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm.
  • By email: send a request to hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, or include your in-game player ID (Settings → Account → Player ID).

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted: your account identifier, display name, save data, game progress, purchase history linked to your account, and support correspondence.

What we may keep: transaction records that we are legally required to retain for tax and commerce purposes (5 years), and aggregated or anonymized statistics that can no longer be linked to you.

What we cannot delete: your Google account itself belongs to Google, not to us. Deleting your data with us removes what we hold; to close the platform account you would need to contact Google directly. If you play more than one of our games, each has its own save data — tell us which ones you want removed, or say "all games".

Deleting your account does not automatically refund unused in-app purchases, and progress cannot be restored afterwards. Please make sure this is what you want.

11. Children

Our games are intended for players aged 14 and over and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14 (or under the minimum age required for consent in your country, where that age is higher).

If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child below the applicable age without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at hello.mega.rabbit@gmail.com.

12. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls limiting who on our side can reach player data, and reliance on Google Cloud infrastructure for storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
